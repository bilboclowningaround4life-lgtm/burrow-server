3. Who can see it

  • Other players see your username, animals, den, bio, Discord username if you add one, badges, when you joined and were last seen, your chat in the room you are in, whether you are currently banned, and your total playtime if you make it public on your account page (it is private unless you choose otherwise).
  • Moderators can also read stored chat, whispers, Jam-A-Grams and wall posts, reports, and ban history, see which other accounts used the same device or signed in from the same IP addresses (never the addresses themselves; for devices, only the hashes described in section 2), and see your total playtime and the overall activity figures. They cannot see your IP address, sign-in history, password or inventory.
  • Moderators can look up which accounts signed in from an IP address they already have, only to stop ban evasion: finding new accounts made by someone who is banned. The search shows only account names and when they signed in from that address, never the address itself or any other address. Every search is recorded under the moderator's name and reviewed by the administrator.
  • The administrator can see everything listed in section 2, and can search accounts by IP address for the same purpose.
  • Our hosting provider stores the data on our behalf, in {{hostedIn}}. If that is outside your country, your data is transferred there to provide the service.
  • Cloudflare carries part of the traffic to this server. See section 4.

We share data with nobody else, unless the law requires it. WildWorks receives nothing from us about you.

4. Cloudflare

Parts of Burrow are reached through Cloudflare, Inc., which protects the server from attacks and delivers pages faster. When your connection passes through Cloudflare, it processes your IP address and technical details of the request (such as browser type, the page requested and the time), and may set a cookie that is needed for security. Cloudflare operates worldwide, so this can happen outside your country, including in the United States.

When you sign in or create an account, a Cloudflare captcha (Turnstile) checks that you are a person. It looks at your IP address and at signals from your browser and device, and only tells us whether the check passed.

Cloudflare gives us overall traffic statistics, such as the number of requests and visitors and the countries they come from. These do not identify you and we do not combine them with your account.

Cloudflare handles this data under its own policy: cloudflare.com/privacypolicy.

6. Your rights

Wherever you live, you can use these on your account page:

  • Access and portability: "Download your data" gives you a file with everything we hold about your account.
  • Correction: change your password, bio, Discord username and settings, or request a new username.
  • Deletion: "Delete account" removes your account for good. This also works while you are banned.

You can also ask us to restrict or stop a use of your data, or ask any question, by writing to {{legalEmail}}. We answer within one month.

7. Children

Burrow is for people aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone younger and we do not offer parental consent. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under that age we delete it. Parents and guardians can ask for this at {{legalEmail}}.

8. Cookies and your device

The website sets only cookies that are needed to keep you signed in: a session cookie on your account page (12 hours) and, if you choose "Trust this device", a 2FA cookie (30 days). The client keeps your saved sign-in on your own device if you tick "Remember me". Cloudflare may add a security cookie of its own (section 4). There are no advertising or tracking cookies.

9. Security

Passwords are stored only as salted hashes, sign-in tokens as hashes, and connections are encrypted when the server address begins with https. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach puts your data at risk we will tell you in the game and on this site.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the version at the top changes with it, and significant changes are announced in the game or on this site before they apply.
